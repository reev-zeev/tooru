# Country and City Data

Purpose: hold declarative geography and operating configuration. Countries, cities, service areas, time zones, currencies, languages, pricing policies, and feature activation belong here as data.

Initial Saudi commercial activation: Riyadh, Jeddah, Makkah, Taif, Madinah, Dammam.

The model must also support the Gulf, Levant, Iraq, Turkey, Egypt, and Yemen without forking application code.
